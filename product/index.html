Clever Role

Digital staff for small businesses. Clever Role handles email, bookings and follow-ups. Replies are prepared as drafts, and nothing is sent without your approval unless you switch on automatic sending for a specific action.

What the service does

  • Reads incoming customer email and sorts enquiries.
  • Prepares reply drafts for you to review.
  • Sends follow-ups you have approved, from your own address.
  • Books, moves and cancels meetings on your calendar.
  • Keeps customer contact details in one place.

Permissions requested when you connect Google or Microsoft 365

  • Profile and email address: to show which account is connected.
  • Email (read, draft, send): to read incoming mail, prepare drafts, and send messages you approved.
  • Calendar events: to book, move and cancel meetings and avoid double-booking.
  • Contacts: to look up your customers' details (read only; Clever Role never changes your contacts).

You choose whether to connect and can disconnect at any time. Nothing is sent or changed without your approval unless you allow a specific action automatically.

Your data

Google and Microsoft data is used only to provide these features. It is never sold and never used for advertising. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
